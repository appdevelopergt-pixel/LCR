Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy describes how Las Carnes de Rodrigo, Guatemala ("we", "us"), collects, uses, stores and protects the information we receive through our internal tool "Social Hub", which connects to our Facebook Page, our Instagram professional account and, when enabled, our WhatsApp Business number.

1. Information we collect

When a person sends a message to our official accounts, the tool receives and stores:

  • The content of direct messages sent to our Facebook Page (Messenger), our Instagram professional account and, when enabled, our WhatsApp Business number.
  • The public profile name or username and the platform-assigned identifier (ID) of the person who writes to us. On WhatsApp, also the phone number from which we are contacted.
  • The date and time of each message and of our replies.
  • Comments left on our own Facebook and Instagram posts (text, public name or username, and date).
  • Aggregated statistics of our own posts (reactions, comments, shares and reach), which do not identify individual people.
  • The ID and username of our own Page and Instagram professional account, to show which accounts are connected.

We do not access passwords, private conversations with third parties, or the personal profiles of customers. We only access messages and comments addressed to our own accounts.

2. How we use the information

We use the information only to receive customer messages and comments in one place and to reply to them manually (customer service, orders and reservations), and to review the performance of our own posts. Replies to messages are sent only in response to messages that the customer started, within the time window allowed by Meta. We do not use the data for advertising, profiling or automated decisions, and we do not sell it.

3. Who has access and where it is stored

Access is limited to authorized staff of Las Carnes de Rodrigo. Data is stored using Google LLC cloud services (data processor), in servers located in the United States and accessed from Guatemala. We do not share, sell or transfer this data to any other third party, except when required by law.

4. Data from Meta

Data received from Meta platforms is used only for the purposes described above and in accordance with Meta's Platform Terms and policies. Access tokens are kept private and are never shared.

5. Retention

Customer messages, comments and related records (such as conversation assignments) are kept for as long as needed to attend to the customer, and no longer than 30 days, after which they are deleted automatically. Technical webhook logs are kept for no longer than 7 days. Aggregated statistics of our own posts do not identify individuals and are kept for internal reporting. You may request earlier deletion at any time (see below).

6. Your rights and data deletion

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data. To request deletion, follow the steps on our Data Deletion Instructions page. You can also remove our app from your Facebook account under Settings → Apps and Websites.

7. Requests from authorities

We only disclose personal data to public authorities when required by a valid legal request, and we limit the disclosure to what the law requires.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
