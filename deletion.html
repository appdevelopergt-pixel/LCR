Data Deletion Instructions

If you would like Las Carnes de Rodrigo to delete the data we received from you through Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp (messages, comments, public name or username, profile identifier and, on WhatsApp, phone number), follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to lascarnesderodrigo@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my data".
  2. Include your name or username as it appears on the social network (or your phone number, if you contacted us through WhatsApp) and the account you used to contact us.
  3. We will delete your messages and comments from our records within a maximum of 30 days and will confirm by email once it is done.

In any case, messages and comments are deleted automatically after 30 days, as described in our Privacy Policy.

You can also remove our app's connection from your Facebook account under Settings → Apps and Websites.

For more information, see our Privacy Policy.
